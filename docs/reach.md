# Reach on Threads

What decides how many people see a post, split by how sure we are. Last checked: October 2026.

## Stated by Meta or Adam Mosseri

- Threads ranks candidate posts with an AI model that predicts each viewer's interest from their
  past activity (Meta Transparency Center).
- The Following feed is chronological and holds only accounts you follow. In November 2024 Mosseri
  said ranking was rebalanced toward followed accounts and away from unconnected recommendations,
  so reach from strangers is harder to get than in 2023.
- Replies: Mosseri has said repeatedly that the sum of your replies is worth about as much as the
  sum of your posts. Answering comments under your own posts is growth work, not chores.
- Links: in mid 2025 Mosseri said Threads had been working on ranking links and that links were
  doing much better. No current rule demotes them.
- Originality: original content is favored over reposted or recycled text.

## Reported by practitioners (unconfirmed)

- Early velocity: how fast the first reactions arrive matters more than the final count. A post
  that stalls in its first hour is capped. Figures such as "30 reactions in the first hour" are
  vendor claims.
- Replies are the heaviest signal, then reposts and shares, then likes. Replies of a few words
  count less than real sentences.
- Topic tags (one per post, picked in the composer) put the post in a topic feed and help the For
  You targeting. Hashtag stuffing does nothing.
- Frequency: guides range from a few posts a week to 3 to 5 a day. None documents a penalty for
  more, but repetitive or templated posts look automated and invite reports.
- A link wrapped in a real thought does better than a bare link.

## Measured with these scripts (one account, about 50 followers, October 2026)

- At one post every 10 to 20 minutes (about 90 a day) the median post got 100 to 200 views; about
  one in 30 passed 5,000 views and one reached 1,000 likes.
- What broke out: first person (son or grandson), one concrete object (a scarf, a notebook, a razor),
  a short line alone near the end, an ending that is a gesture, not a moral. Relatable control at
  home ("the food was always what he liked") drew the most replies.
- What stayed under 200 views: third person with invented names, stories after the event with no
  object, posts opening with a label such as "Part 1.", question polls, and posts with a sales link
  in the body.
